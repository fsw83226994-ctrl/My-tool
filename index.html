Field aid — not a contract document. Compiled from the BCR&P Manual Vols I–II (Bridge Construction Memos) and the 2024 Standard Specifications (project copies, Aug 2026). BCMs are guidance: the contract, special provisions, and the current published memos govern. The Soldier Pile Wall flow, the troubleshooting trees, and the soldier-pile section steps cite their source and printed page — open the document and check it. The remaining section pages are being brought up to the same standard and are not yet cited line by line; treat an uncited line as a prompt to go read the specification, not as a quotation from it. This tool sets the scope of work and what to look for; it records nothing, replaces no form, and is not a substitute for reading the specification when something matters.
